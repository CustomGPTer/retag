Retag has no paid version, no subscription and no adverts. Other well-known photo metadata apps pay their way with adverts, or keep some of what they do for a paid version.

Which photos it works on

Retag changes JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, DNG, PSD and JPEG XL files, and reads camera RAW, BMP and ICO files. Banana Studio's listing says its app reads and writes the details in JPEG and PNG files and converts HEIC and AVIF to JPEG or PNG. MUDRAVA's listing names JPEG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, WebP and GIF, with TIFF for viewing and cleaning. Syrupy's listing does not name the file types it works on.

Where the work is done

Retag reads and changes your photos on your phone, with or without an internet connection, and no photo is ever sent anywhere. The map is the one part that needs the internet. If where the work is done matters to you, check each app's privacy policy before you give it a photo.

What Retag does not do

  • It works on pictures only. It does not open video or audio files.
  • It does not alter the picture. Anything you can see in a photo stays, as does a watermark that is part of the picture itself.
  • Camera RAW files can have details changed or blanked but not added, and they keep their make, model and maker notes so that they still open properly.
  • A cleaned HEIC or AVIF file is no smaller than before, and keeps some built-in extras such as a depth map. Content Credentials are removed from JPEG, PNG and WebP files, but not yet from HEIC or AVIF.
  • BMP and ICO files have no place to hold details, so there is nothing in them to change.
