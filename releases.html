Release notes

What changed in each version of Retag. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.1 22 November 2026Latest

  • Drag the pin on the location map to move it. The pin lifts clear of your finger so you can see its point, and the latitude and longitude follow it as it moves.
  • Folder buttons along the bottom of the photo map. Tick one or more to show only the photos in those folders. Your choice is remembered.
  • The photo map now shows each place as a small photo on a pin, with a count where several photos share it.
  • Fixed: the search box on the location map was hidden behind the map.

Version 1.0 15 November 2026

  • A full view of what is stored in a photo, grouped in plain words, with a second view that lists every raw tag.
  • Safe to share, which removes everything personal in one tap and keeps the rotation and colour profile. Also Remove everything, Location only, Device only, Dates only, Names and captions, and presets of your own.
  • Editing for dates, camera, lens, exposure, title, caption, author, copyright and keywords, and for any single tag.
  • Location on an OpenStreetMap map: drop a pin, search for a place, use where you are, type coordinates or pick a saved place. Height and compass direction as well.
  • 40 phone profiles for iPhone, Galaxy and Pixel models, a Surprise me button, and profiles of your own.
  • Dates: set any date field, shift by an amount, take the date from the file name, or match the file's own date.
  • Bulk work: tick many photos or a whole folder and clean, edit, re-date, relocate, rename or export them together.
  • Copying details from one photo to others, and saved templates of changes.
  • A privacy scan that lists the photos carrying a location, a name or a serial number.
  • A photo map, a side-by-side compare, and export as text, CSV or JSON.
  • A HEIC, HEIF and AVIF to JPEG converter that carries the details across.
  • JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, DNG, PSD and JPEG XL files can be changed. Camera RAW, BMP and ICO files can be viewed.
  • Photos from the album list, the file browser, your phone's picker, or sent from another app with Share. Share clean sends a cleaned copy straight on.
  • Copies saved to Pictures/Retag by default, with a switch to change the original instead and an Undo.
  • Blue or wallpaper colours, light or dark.
  • A notice when a newer version is out.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.1 is called Retag.apk. It is 4.5 MB (4,536,640 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

db90f123d1cf213094164462d64c9a81576dd4de1e79ed0bae84aafa4246657c

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Retag.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
