Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This policy covers the Retag app for Android (package uk.jj.retag) and this website. Retag is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your photos are read and changed on your phone. They are never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Retag has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.
  • The map, the place search and the height look-up come from public services on the internet. Using them tells those services which part of the map you are looking at, or the place you searched for. The detail is below.

What stays on your phone

Retag reads a photo where it is kept and, by default, saves the changed copy to the Pictures/Retag folder, where your gallery and other apps can see it. You can choose instead to save the copy beside the original, or to change the original itself.

Your settings, saved places, remove presets, templates and phone profiles are kept in the app's private storage. If you change an original, Retag holds the earlier version in its private storage for a short while so that you can undo, and then clears it. A photo leaves your phone only when you choose to send it somewhere yourself, with Share.

What the app can reach

All files on your phoneTo list your albums and folders, read the details in your photos and write your changes back, in whichever folder a photo is keptOnce you allow it, which Android asks the first time you open Retag. Retag looks only for pictures

Retag does not ask for your contacts, your camera or your microphone.

Services the app contacts

These services also receive your device's internet address, as any online service does, and the name and version of the app. OpenStreetMap's services are run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation under its privacy policy. Open-Meteo sets out how it handles requests in its terms and privacy notes. The developer of Retag receives none of this.

If you would rather the map services saw nothing, type the coordinates in by hand with your phone offline. Viewing, editing and cleaning photos need no internet connection at all.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Photos in Pictures/Retag stay until you delete them from your gallery.
  • Saved places, presets, templates and phone profiles stay until you delete them under Saved items or Phone profiles.
  • Pictures of the map are kept for a while in the app's temporary storage so that the map loads faster. Clearing the app's cache removes them.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Retag deletes everything it holds. Photos you saved stay on your phone.
  • If your phone's own backup is switched on, Android may include Retag's settings and saved items in it. That backup is yours and is managed in your phone's settings.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Retag holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your photos are on your own phone, where you control them directly using the steps above. For requests made to the map services, your rights are exercised with those services. If you need help, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

The app's own data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read. Photos you save are ordinary files in your phone's shared storage, as they were before.

The app's connections to GitHub, OpenStreetMap and Open-Meteo use HTTPS.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

Retag is a general-purpose photo tool. It is not directed at children and its developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact

For a question about this policy or about your data, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details or photos in one.
